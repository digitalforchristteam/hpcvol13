Free ways to use WhatsApp

Most efficient: one WhatsApp group per ministry or event. Tap Share on an event to post it, add a WhatsApp poll for Yes or No, and message only the people who have not replied.

Also free: one-to-one WhatsApp buttons, Communities and Channels. Meta's automatic business service now charges per message, so we do not use it.

Your data and privacy

This is the front-end first release. Data is stored in your own browser, so it is not yet shared between people. Accounts, shared database, roles and email reminders come next, using Cloudflare’s free tools.
